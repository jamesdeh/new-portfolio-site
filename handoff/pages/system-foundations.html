I like the unglamorous end of security: reading configs, writing threat models and fixing the boring thing before it becomes the interesting thing.

Body copy sits in a 68-character column. It is set in Geist at 18px with a 1.6 line height, so a long Log post stays easy on the eyes in Noir and in Paper.

Small text carries captions, excerpts and footnotes. It never drops below 15px.
